Map the jeep you ride

Para is a map of Metro Manila's jeepney routes, drawn from the rides of the people who take them. The Para app records your ride, and once it's checked, your route is on the map for every commuter.

On Android

Download the app and install it. It's free.

Download the Android app
  1. Open the downloaded file, para-contributor.apk.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Para and make an account.

On iPhone

There's no iPhone app yet. You can record a ride in Safari instead.

Record in the browser
  1. Make an account, and allow location when Safari asks.
  2. Keep Para open with the screen on until you get off. Para can't follow the ride while the phone is locked or another app is open.
  3. To find Para again, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
