# 01: Check the Supabase plan's limits

Status: ready-for-human (owner: read the plan's limits in the Supabase dashboard and write them below)
Type: task
Who: the owner

## What to do

In the Supabase dashboard (the project's **Settings → Billing** / **Usage** pages), write down:

- the plan (Free, Pro, …);
- **monthly active users** allowed, and this month's count;
- **database size** allowed, and today's size;
- **egress** (data sent out) allowed, and this month's amount;
- anything the Usage page shows close to its limit.

Then estimate how many of the 100,000 will sign in as contributors in a month. Commuters don't sign in and don't count.

## Acceptance checks

- The numbers are written under `## Comments` with the date.
- If the expected contributors, or the database's growth over a year of recordings, come within half of a limit, the hub writes a follow-up ticket (a plan upgrade, or thinning old recordings).
