# 07: Walk versus ride, and several jeepneys in one recording

Status: needs-info (owner: a product decision)
Type: product decision
Who: the owner
Source: the owner's phone test of the app, 2026-10-26, feedback (b)

A real trip is often: walk to the stop, ride one jeepney, walk, ride a second. The recorder has one continuous trail and the Papunta/Pabalik way button; it knows nothing of walking or of a second vehicle. A recording that mixes them gives the studio a "route" that is partly a walk and partly two routes.

## Options

1. **One recording = one jeepney ride** (recommended for now). Start when you board, Stop when you get off. A second jeepney is a second recording. Simplest to explain, and the studio can trust the whole trail.
2. **A Sakay/Baba (board/alight) marker** inside one recording: the contributor taps when they board and when they get off; only the part between is the ride, the rest is a walk. It reuses the Mark button's idea (a pin on the trail), but its meaning is new, so it needs the owner's design and wording.
3. **Auto-detection of walking vs riding** from speed and stops. Later: it is a guess, it will be wrong at traffic and at stops, and it should only suggest a split for the contributor to confirm.

## Recommendation

Do option 1 now and say it in the first-run copy (ticket 11). Keep option 2 as the next step if testers still mix walks in. Option 3 only after real recordings show what the speeds look like.

## Questions for the owner

- Is "one recording = one jeepney" acceptable for the testers?
- If Sakay/Baba is wanted: the words, the look, and whether one recording can hold several rides or the markers only trim the walk at both ends.
- What does the studio do today with a walk at the ends of a recording?
