# 11: When to press Start

Status: needs-info (owner: copy)
Type: product decision
Who: the owner
Source: the owner's phone test of the app, 2026-10-26, feedback (f)

Nothing tells a first-time tester when to press Start: at home, at the stop, when the jeepney comes, or once on board. A recording that starts early is mostly a walk or a wait, and the studio cannot tell (ticket 07).

## Recommendation

**Press Start when you board. Press Stop when you get off.** One recording is one jeepney ride for now (ticket 07).

## Where it could be said

The owner's call, with Figma: the Ready screen (above Start), the consent text, the tester message, or all. No wording is drafted here: the copy and its place are the owner's.

## Questions for the owner

- Is "start when you board" right for the testers?
- Where should it be said, and in which words (English, Filipino or both)?
