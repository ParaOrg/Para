# Release of 2026-11-25: staging into main

`staging` at #1202 (`a9e0b53`) into `main`. `main` was last released yesterday (#1188); since then it took only the nightly Publish map run of 2026-11-25 03:22 UTC, whose files `staging` already holds (`128fb9c`). A trial merge of `staging` into `main` is clean and leaves exactly `staging`'s tree. 16 merged pull requests, #1171 and #1187 to #1202.

This release is early, by the owner's choice, so the Publish map fix (#1201) is on `main` before tonight's nightly run.

- **Commuters at www.para-commute.org still see Coming soon** (#1127). The commuter map's changes below go live on `main` but stay behind Coming soon. Staging and every Preview show the map.

## On screen

### Commuter map (behind Coming soon on production)

- **Two cards while nothing is open** (#1192): "Ways to explore our app" and "Help make Para better", along the bottom on a phone and top left on a wide screen, as the owner's frames draw them. Not closable. Donate is drawn and disabled until a donation page exists.
