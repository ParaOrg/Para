## Why

A first-timer doesn't know how to stop a jeep, pay or get off. Sakay.ph answers some of it in its FAQ, and JeepTa has a "Riding Tips" page. Para has nothing yet. The tips cost no data and no backend.

## The tips (draft text)

1. Read the signboard on the front and side. It names the route's ends and the streets it passes.
2. Wave it down where people wait. Some places, like UP's campuses, only allow loading at marked stops.
3. Pay once you're seated. Pass your fare forward and say where you got on and where you're getting off.
4. Students, seniors and PWDs get a discount. Show your ID when you pay.
5. Carry coins and small bills. A ₱500 or ₱1,000 bill is hard to change on a jeep.
6. Say "Para po" or knock on the roof when you're near your stop.
7. On a long route, tell the driver early where you're getting off.
8. Keep your bag in front of you, not on your back.
9. If there's space, sit on the left side, so people on the roadside can see the jeep still has room.
10. The plate number and route are painted on the side. Note them if you need to report something.
11. Traffic is worse on paydays (the 15th and the last day of the month). Leave earlier.
12. Ask the driver or other riders when you're unsure. Most are glad to help.

Fares aren't in the tips. Para already works them out from the current LTFRB table (`src/features/routes/model/fares.ts`), and the notes' older fare rule is out of date.
